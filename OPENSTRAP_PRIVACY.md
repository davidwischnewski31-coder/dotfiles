# Privacy Policy for OpenStrap Local Import

Effective date: July 23, 2026

OpenStrap Local Import is a private, personal-use tool operated by David Wischnewski. It is used to retrieve and store the operator's own WHOOP data.

## Data accessed

With the user's explicit authorization through WHOOP OAuth, the tool may access:

- Basic WHOOP profile information
- Body measurements
- Physiological cycles and strain
- Recovery metrics, including resting heart rate and heart rate variability
- Sleep records and sleep-stage summaries
- Workout records and heart-rate-zone summaries

## How data is used

The data is used only to import, display, and analyze the authorized user's personal health and fitness history.

## Storage and sharing

Imported data and authorization credentials are stored locally on devices controlled by the user. The operator does not sell, rent, advertise with, or share this data with third parties. The tool does not operate a public analytics or advertising service.

WHOOP processes data according to its own privacy policy when providing the WHOOP API and OAuth authorization service.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains on the user's local device until the user deletes the imported files or application data. The user can revoke the tool's WHOOP access through their WHOOP account or by deleting the authorization in WHOOP.

## Security

Reasonable local security measures are used to protect authorization credentials and imported data. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the tool's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
